ABOUT BFSI SIG

A community that turns conversation into momentum.

BFSINEXT brings together leaders from banking, financial services, insurance, fintech and policy. This one-day forum goes beyond technology and trend hype to focus on how innovation can truly build inclusion, resilience and trust in the financial ecosystem.

We create a trusted space for bankers, insurers, fintech builders, investors, regulators and academics to meet as peers, share perspectives and strengthen India’s financial future.
