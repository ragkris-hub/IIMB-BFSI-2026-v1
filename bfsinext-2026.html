ABOUT BFSI 2026

A full day for the people moving finance forward.

This year’s theme—“Reimagining BFSI: Leading India’s Intelligent Future.”—sets the tone for conversations about the future of financial services: AI, customer expectations, risks and regulations, talent challenges and other moving dynamics.

It is a full-day event for leaders to network, collaborate, deliberate and influence initiatives— accelerating value realisation across the ecosystem.

Delegates are a generous mix from global and Indian banks, insurance and financial-services companies, startups, government and industry bodies, academia and students.
